Privacy Policy

Tiled Verandah Ops · Last updated 7 November 2026

Tiled Verandah Ops is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Tiled Verandah YouTube channel to run the channel's operations. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app accesses only the owner's own account, to:

  • Google Sheets: read and update the channel's content tracker and business ledger.
  • Google Drive: download finished videos for upload and save receipts and licence documents.
  • Gmail: read emails the owner has labelled as receipts, and send reports and reminders to the owner.
  • YouTube and YouTube Analytics: upload the owner's videos, set video settings, and read the channel's own performance statistics.

How data is used

Data is used only to run the channel's operations for the owner: planning content, preparing video descriptions, recording business expenses and income, and reporting channel performance. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described below.

Some text (such as content ideas, video details, channel statistics and the text of receipt emails) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft content and extract receipt details. Exchange rates are fetched from a public currency-rate service; no personal data is sent to it.

Where data is stored

Data stays in the owner's own Google account (Sheets, Drive, Gmail, YouTube) and in the automation software running on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tiled Verandah Ops' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
